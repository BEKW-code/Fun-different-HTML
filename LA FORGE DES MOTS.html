Une table is feminine grammar. The table has no biological gender. Nobody has to give the furniture a princess crown. 👑

Why « du », « de la », « de l’ »?

For indefinite amounts of food or substances: du pain (some bread, masculine), de la soupe (some soup, feminine), de l’eau (some water, before a vowel sound). But du can also mean de + le (“from the / of the”), depending on the sentence. Context wins.

What about « au » and « aux »?

à + le → au (au marché). à + les → aux (aux jardins). Similarly, de + le → du and de + les → des.

Why no « un/une » in Vaeltharyn?

Our other language, Zoé's Vaeltharyn, does not require articles or grammatical gender in the same way. French is a different historical system: article use and agreement are part of its structure, not mistakes to remove.

⚔ Article duel: un or une?

01 • Number spellcraft

70 = 60 + 10

Soixante-dix is how standard metropolitan French builds seventy. At eighty, it's four twenties. Yes, really.

02 • Color agreement

Bleu → Bleue

Un pull bleu; une robe bleue. The color adjective agrees with the noun, not the object's supposed personality.

03 • Everyday verbs

J'ai faim.

French says “I have hunger” rather than “I am hungry.” Different language, different construction. Learn the pattern.

04 • Necessary profanity

PUTAIN!

One gloriously versatile, vulgar exclamation. A triumph, a disaster, a dropped wrench. Learn context before deploying at dinner.
